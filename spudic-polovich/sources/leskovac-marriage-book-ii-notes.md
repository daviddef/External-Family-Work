Method: FamilySearch image viewer, 3x zoom, panned page by page (right page then left page, top to bottom). Image numbers are the viewer's "Image N of 644". Each image is a two-page spread. ## KEY FINDING — image 492 (1850 – 1851), read at high zoom

Format note: from about 1849 the entries are in Croatian, with house numbers ("Nº 7"). In each entry the groom is labelled "Mladenac" (bridegroom) and the bride "Divojka" (maiden/bride), so "Mladenac" and "Divojka" are NOT surnames (the "Dvojk-" reading on first sight is "Divojka"). Each entry gives the person's first name, father (sin pokojnoga = son of the late), house number, and "krajišnik Satnie Barilovićke" (border soldier of the Barilović company).

**Left page, last entry (bottom of the page): "1851, 2ᵃ ožujka [2 March 1851], Leskovac"**
- Groom: "Vid, [Mladenac] sin pokojnoga Janka Spudića iz Leskovca Nº 3(?) krajišnik Satnie Barilovićke" — Vid, son of the LATE Janko Spudić of Leskovac (house number read as 3; handwriting uncertain). Age column reads 20(?).
- Bride: "i Jele [Divojka], kći Mike Ar[…] i Julića/Julije obudovjele, Leskovačke" — Jele (Jelena), daughter of Mika Ar[šulić?] and Julija, widow, of Leskovac. Age 20. The surname is cut short at "Ar" on the page; it is consistent with Aršulić.
- Witnesses: "Jure Spudić iz Leskovca Nº 17" and "Pave Tergović iz Barilovića Nº 8, obdvaj(?) krajišnik Satnie Barilovićke". Priest: "Ladislav Jagodić, župnik mesta".
- This matches the Family Tree event (Vid Spudić m. 2 Mar 1851 Jelena Aršulić). It gives the groom's father as the late **Janko (Ivan) Spudić**, NOT Stephen/Stjepan. Caveats: age column reads 20 (not 23 as a 8 Jan 1828 birth would give); house number is 3 (uncertain), not 10. Needs a second look at higher resolution before it is treated as settled.

**Right page, first entry: "1851, 2ᵃ ožujka [2 March 1851], Leskovac"** (same day as above)
- Groom: "Miko [Mladenac], sin Vida Spudića iz Leskovca Nº 7, krajišnik Satnie Barilovićke" — Miko, son of Vid Spudić of house 7. Age 20.
- Bride: "Jane [Divojka] kći pokojnog Ivana Spudića, obudovjela? … Leskovačka" — Jana, daughter of the late Ivan Spudić (wording after "obu…" unclear).
- Witnesses: "Jure Spudić iz Leskovca Nº 15" and "Miko Spudić Nº 4, obveznik krajišnik Satnie Barilovićke".

**Right page, other entries** (not Spudić): 9 Lipnja? 1851 "Jure Pottović(?) [or Polović — t/l unclear] udovac iz Gerčaka(?) Nº 42(?), krajišnik Satnie Svarča(?)" + "Jane ostavljena udova pokojnoga Jose Bellavića, iz …, Leskovačka" (ages 42 / 38) — surname flag: could be Polović, needs a closer look; 9 Lipnja? 1851 "Pave Matković udovac iz …Nº 8, krajišnik Satnie Barilovićke" + "Mare Jurčević udova obdovljena? tuge? Leskovačka" (53 / 45).

**Left page, earlier entries** (none Spudić; read in the same pass): 1850 Lipnja "Marko Lukić(?), udovac iz Velimerice(?) Nº 7" + "Dore ostavljena udova pokojnoga Bote(?) Bo…ića iz Vrbice(?)
